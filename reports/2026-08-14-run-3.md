## Findings

### Bugbot is included on Cursor Enterprise

Verified https://cursor.com/pricing for the first time. Bugbot is on usage-based billing
across Individual and Teams, with on-demand usage "billed in arrears" once the included
amount is consumed. That confirms the assumption two public cost calculators rest on.

The qualifier the calculators do not carry: Enterprise lists "agentic code reviews with
Bugbot" as an included feature. Against a Cursor Enterprise account the metered-cost
argument does not apply at all. Worth a qualifying question before the cost pitch runs.

Registered as `bugbot-usage-billing`, severity critical, because a reversal here removes
the wedge and nothing else in the file would notice.

### Graphite has no usable release feed

Verified https://graphite.com/blog. The anchor is present, so the fetch is healthy, but
the newest post is 2026-04-01, four months old, and the posts are launch announcements
rather than release notes. Combined with the absence of a changelog page, Graphite
cannot be tracked by date at all.

Demoted from tier 2 to tier 3 and removed from the digest, on the grounds that the feed
does not work, not that the competitor stopped mattering. A silent Graphite in a weekly
digest would have read as "shipped nothing" indefinitely.

Two of their recent posts involve Cursor ("Introducing Cursor Cloud Agents in Graphite",
2026-03-02, and a December company post about building with Cursor). Inference, not
fact: there may be a partnership worth understanding before positioning against either.
