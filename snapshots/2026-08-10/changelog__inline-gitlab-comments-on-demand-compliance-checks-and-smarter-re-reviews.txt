 Inline GitLab Comments & Compliance 
 Acadia 2.2.7 — line-specific GitLab comments, on-demand compliance checks, context-aware follow-ups that recall prior feedback, and a major AI upgrade. 

Acadia 2.2.7: Smarter Reviews, Sharper Context, Stronger Foundations. This release brings major upgrades to how Optibot reviews, reasons, and learns from your codebase. From inline GitLab comments to intelligent re-reviews, every feature was designed to make feedback faster, more relevant, and right where it matters.

 New Features
 Inline Comments for GitLab

No more hunting through general feedback threads. Optibot now leaves contextual, line-specific comments directly in your GitLab merge requests — making it easier to understand and act on feedback instantly.

 On-Demand Compliance & Release Notes (GitLab)

Ask Optibot to "check compliance" or "provide release notes" and it will instantly analyze your MR for compliance gaps or summarize your release changes. AI-powered documentation, right inside your workflow.

 Context-Aware Follow-Up Reviews (GitLab + GitHub)

Optibot now remembers what's been fixed, acknowledged, or 👍 reacted to — avoiding repeated suggestions and focusing only on new or unresolved issues. Every re-review gets smarter.

 Improvements
 More Accurate Code Search (GitLab + GitHub)

We've upgraded Optibot's search stack to a new hybrid method that improves accuracy, reduces false assumptions, and delivers more grounded review comments.

 Automatic Knowledge Base Updates (GitLab + GitHub)

Optibot now keeps itself up-to-date. When you push to main, its understanding of your repository refreshes automatically — ensuring every comment reflects the latest code.

 Under the Hood
 Major AI Framework Upgrade
 A foundational update that enhances performance, reliability, and precision across all agents.
 Next-Generation Agent Architecture
 Completely rebuilt with a modular design for greater efficiency and future-proof scalability — setting the stage for more advanced agent capabilities ahead.
