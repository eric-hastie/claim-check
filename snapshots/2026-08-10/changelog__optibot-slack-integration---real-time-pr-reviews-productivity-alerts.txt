 Optibot + Slack: PR Reviews & Alerts

 Optibot + Slack Integration

This week's release brings one of our most requested features: Optibot now integrates directly with Slack. With one click, you can receive real-time updates on everything Optibot is doing inside your codebase — without leaving your team's workflow.

 What's New
 Real-Time PR Review Notifications
 Optibot now posts directly into your Slack channels the moment it completes a code review.
 These aren't generic "LGTM" alerts — they include Optibot's high-context, high-quality comments built from full-file understanding and repo-level awareness.
 Developer Productivity Insights (Live)
 Get notified when your team's engineering performance improves.
 Optibot will alert you when key metrics move — especially cycle-time reductions — so you can see productivity wins as they happen.
 Seamless Setup
 Connect your Slack workspace and GitHub org in minutes.
 Once connected, Optibot automatically streams insights, reviews, and improvements into your chosen channels.
 Why This Matters
 Engineering leaders get instant visibility.
 Developers stay in flow.
 And your team sees the impact of improvements in real time, without digging through dashboards.

Head to Integrations → Slack inside Optimal AI to connect your workspace.
