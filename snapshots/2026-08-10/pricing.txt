 Frequently Asked Questions

 What counts as a "deep PR review"? A deep PR review is a full AI-powered code review with complete codebase context — Optibot reads across your repos, understands dependencies, and catches bugs that a line-by-line review would miss. It's not just a lint check; it's a full reasoning pass on your PR.

 What is Optibot Insights? Insights is Optimal AI's engineering productivity platform — included on Pro and Max. It tracks PR cycle times, code review times, deployment frequency, AI code ratio, and contributor and agent productivity. You can also ask Optibot natural language questions about your engineering data directly.

 Does my free trial start on the Max plan? Yes. Every Optibot trial starts on the Max plan so you can experience the full platform — multi-repo context, every sub-agent, the security agent, and Insights — with no caps. When the trial ends, you can choose the plan that fits your team's velocity.

 What happens when I hit my monthly review limit? You're downgraded to quick reviews — local, lighter scans that still catch some issues but without the full codebase context of a deep review. Deep reviews reset at the start of your next billing cycle, and you can upgrade your plan at any time to restore them immediately.

 Can I try Optibot before paying? Yes, all plans come with a free trial. No credit card required to get started.

 Do I need to buy a seat for every engineer on my team? Yes, seats should reflect your full team size. Optibot's pricing is per user, and review limits are calculated per user per month — so buying fewer seats than you have engineers will impact the quality and limits available to your team.

 What's the difference between CI fixing and the Optibot code fixer agent? CI fixing (available on Plus) automatically re-runs and attempts to fix failed CI checks. The Optibot code fixer agent (Pro and Max) goes deeper — it actively rewrites and fixes the code in your PR, not just the CI pipeline.

 What is the Optibot security agent? The security agent runs full codebase security scans, checks against the CWE/CVE database for the latest known vulnerabilities, and flags issues at the PR level before they ever hit production. It's included on Pro and Max plans.

 Can I switch plans or add seats later? Yes, you can upgrade or add seats at any time — changes take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of your billing cycle. Plan tier is set at the org level, so a plan change applies to everyone in your organization rather than per-seat.
