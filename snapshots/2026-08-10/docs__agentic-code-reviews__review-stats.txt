 Review Statistics

 Review Statistics gives you a live view of how much reviewing is happening across your organization, broken down by member, repository, and time period.

 Where to Find It

 Navigate to Repo Insights → Review Stats in the left sidebar.

 Summary Cards

 Three headline numbers appear at the top of the page and update whenever you change the date range:

 Card What it shows 
 Total Reviews Number of Optibot reviews completed in the selected period 
 Avg Reviews / Member Total reviews divided by the number of active members 
 Top Repository The repository with the highest review volume in the period 

 Each card also shows a percentage change compared to the previous period of the same length.

 Reviews Over Time

 A bar chart shows review volume across the selected date range. Granularity adjusts automatically based on how wide your range is. A dashed reference line marks the average so you can quickly spot periods above or below your normal pace.

 Reviews by Member

 A paginated table lists each seated team member alongside their review count for the selected period. The member with the most reviews is highlighted with a “Most Active” label.

 Search and sort

 Search : filter by member name using the search box in the top-right of the table

 Reviews column : click the column header to toggle between highest-to-lowest and lowest-to-highest order

 The rank column (# 1, 2, 3…) is only shown when sorted highest to lowest.

 Troubleshooting

 Issue Fix 
 No data showing Ensure the date range covers a period when Optibot was active on your repositories 
 Member missing from the table The table shows seated members only. Check that the member has an assigned Optibot seat in Organization Settings. 
 No data in the chart Check that the date range overlaps with when reviews were completed; the chart reflects the same period as the summary cards
