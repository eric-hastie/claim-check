 Optibot — V2.0.5 Acadia Release

Optibot V2.0.5 – "Acadia" is all about cross-platform support and performance foundation-building. The big story: Optibot now works beyond GitHub — it's officially integrated with GitLab, and the core engine has been rebuilt to handle larger, more complex reviews faster and smarter.

 Features
 GitLab Integration is Here!

You can now connect your GitLab accounts (both GitLab.com and self-hosted instances) to Optibot. This brings our powerful AI-driven code reviews, summaries, and analytics to your GitLab Merge Requests. The integration includes:

 A simple setup process to connect your GitLab account.
 The ability to select and install Optibot on your GitLab projects.
 Full support for automated reviews and summaries on your Merge Requests.
 Improvements
 New Email & Password Login for GitLab Users

To better support our new integrations, you can now sign up and log in using your email and password, providing a new alternative to GitHub authentication.

 Unified Organization View

Your dashboard has been updated to seamlessly display all your organizations and repositories from both GitHub and GitLab in a single, unified view.

 More Reliable Reviews for Large Pull Requests

We've improved our AI's ability to handle very large and complex pull requests. The system is now smarter at processing extensive changes, reducing failures and ensuring you get a complete review every time.

 Under the Hood Improvements
 Re-architected Repository Context Engine

We've completely rebuilt our core engine for analyzing and understanding your codebases. This new architecture is faster, more reliable, and is the foundation that allows us to support multiple platforms like GitLab. You'll notice faster initial repository setup and more accurate context for your reviews.

 Enhanced Performance and Stability

We've made significant upgrades to our backend infrastructure, resulting in a faster, more stable, and more scalable platform. These changes ensure a smoother experience and lay the groundwork for exciting new features to come.
